**Supply Chain & Inventory Analysis Dashboard**

**Project Overview**

This project is an interactive Supply Chain & Inventory Analysis Dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, inventory status, demand trends, and warehouse efficiency. It helps businesses make data-driven decisions to optimize inventory and improve supply chain operations.

**Objectives**

Analyze total sales and product demand

Monitor inventory levels and stock status

Identify high-demand and low-stock products

Compare warehouse and regional performance

Support efficient inventory and supply chain planning

**Tools & Technologies Used**

Power BI – Data visualization & dashboard creation

Excel / CSV Dataset – Data source

DAX – Calculated measures and KPIs

**Key Features**

Interactive filters (Date, Category, Sub-Category, Region)

KPI cards for Sales, Quantity, Demand, Inventory Turnover, Returns

Stock status analysis (Low Stock vs Optimal Stock)

Category-wise and product-wise sales analysis

Warehouse performance comparison

Year-wise demand trend visualization

**Dashboard Insights**

Identifies products at risk of stockouts

Highlights fast-moving and high-revenue categories

Reveals warehouse efficiency differences

Shows growth trends in customer demand over time

**Conclusion**

This dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of supply chain and inventory performance, enabling organizations to reduce stock issues, improve demand forecasting, and enhance overall operational efficiency.
